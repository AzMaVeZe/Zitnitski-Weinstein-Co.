Classification guide
  1. No election filed within 3 months of incorporation → Regular (23% corporate rate; dividends taxed again at shareholder level).
  2. Election made + all shareholders are relatives → Family — income attributed to the representative shareholder at individual progressive rates.
  3. Election made + unrelated shareholders + 20 or fewer + holds only Israeli real estate → House — same attribution; individual tracks A, B, and C all available.
